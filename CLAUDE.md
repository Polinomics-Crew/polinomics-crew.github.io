# Standing instructions for the Polinomics routine (set by the site owner)

- Publish a fresh edition every day. Do not skip a day because sources are thin: publish whatever you can verify (aim for 12 to 16 stories, but fewer is fine, minimum about 6). Never invent facts.
- BBC, Reuters, Guardian, AP, FT and Sky pages are not reachable. WebSearch restricted with allowed_domains to cnbc.com, euronews.com, tradingeconomics.com, ons.gov.uk and gov.uk works well and returns detailed snippets.
- Every edition must include a stock market story (topic "Economy", region "United States") saying whether the S&P 500 closed up or down on the latest trading day, with the percentage move, closing level, and the Dow and Nasdaq. Put it as order 2 or 3. Use the last completed session; say if a session is still in progress.
- Keep all reading levels a similar length (aim 90 to 150 words), even the simplest ones.

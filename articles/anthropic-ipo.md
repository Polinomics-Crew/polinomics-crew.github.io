I Wouldn't Buy the Anthropic IPO
By: Wilf Brown

My problem with Anthropic's IPO is the price. I can believe Claude will become more useful, that businesses will spend heavily on it, and that Anthropic will grow into a huge company. I still struggle to see why I should buy shares at a valuation approaching $2 trillion.

At that price, an awful lot has to go right.

The growth is impressive. But investors are buying what comes next, and the expectations leave little room for an ordinary bad quarter. A slowdown that would be perfectly manageable for the business could be brutal for the share price.

Then there's the cost of staying competitive. Anthropic has to keep spending on computing power and development while rivals try to win the same customers. Being ahead today offers little comfort if defending that position keeps getting more expensive.

I'm also sceptical of the assumption that businesses will become permanently loyal to one AI provider. They have every reason to compare prices, use several services and switch when something better comes along. That makes future profits harder to predict than the excitement suggests.

The shares might jump on their first day. I wouldn't be surprised. A heavily anticipated listing can attract plenty of buyers before anyone starts asking difficult questions.

My bet is that the disappointment comes later, when strong results turn out to be insufficient to justify the price people paid.

I like the product. I wouldn't buy the IPO.
